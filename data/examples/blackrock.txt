About this role

BlackRock's Analyst Program is a two-year program for graduating students
beginning full time in the summer following graduation. Analysts join one of
several business areas across the Americas region, including Aladdin Engineering,
Risk and Quantitative Analysis, Portfolio Management Group, and Client Businesses.

Aladdin is the technology platform that underpins the firm's investment process,
bringing risk analytics, portfolio construction, and trading onto a single system.
Analysts placed in Risk and Quantitative Analysis work alongside portfolio
managers on risk measurement, scenario analysis, and the quantitative models that
sit behind portfolio construction decisions.

Responsibilities
- Support portfolio managers with risk measurement and exposure reporting
- Contribute to quantitative research on systematic strategies and signals
- Build and maintain tooling in Python; exposure to C++ is a plus
- Communicate analytical results clearly to investment professionals

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree expected between December 2026 and June 2027
- Coursework in finance, economics, mathematics, statistics, or computer science
- Demonstrated quantitative ability and comfort working with data
- Strong written and verbal communication

Compensation: $110,000 base salary

Application deadline: November 14, 2026
Program begins: July 2027
