Investment Intern
Ashcombe Trust
Hudson, New York

Ashcombe Trust is the investment office of the Ashcombe Foundation, which funds
secondary education and rural health care in the Hudson Valley. The office
invests the long-term pool across public equity, fixed income, private capital
and real assets, and runs an endowment model with an external manager structure.
It is a leading limited partner in domestic growth equity funds and has stayed
with several of its managers since their first fund.

The Investment Intern supports the team in manager research and ongoing due
diligence across asset classes. Interns sit with the team, read what the team
reads, and contribute to the written record that goes to the Investment Committee.

What you will do
- Assist with manager research: performance attribution, peer comparison, and
  review of offering documents
- Maintain and improve the data the team relies on for asset allocation work
- Draft sections of investment memoranda and prepare materials for the
  Investment Committee
- Follow markets and summarize developments relevant to the long-term pool

What we look for
- Rising junior or senior pursuing a degree in finance, economics, accounting,
  or a quantitative field
- Comfort with spreadsheets and an interest in institutional investing
- Careful writing; the office runs on memos
- Curiosity about how capital gets allocated at a foundation

Hours: approximately 15 hours per week during the academic year
Pay: $20/hr

Applications due October 14, 2026
