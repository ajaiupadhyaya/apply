About this role

Quillon's Analyst Program is a two-year program for graduating students,
beginning full time in the summer after graduation. Analysts join one of
several groups across the Americas: Sextant Engineering, Risk and Quantitative
Analysis, Portfolio Construction, and Client Solutions.

Sextant is the platform the firm runs its investment process on, bringing risk
analytics, portfolio construction and order execution onto a single system.
Analysts placed in Risk and Quantitative Analysis work alongside portfolio
managers on risk measurement, scenario analysis, and the factor model that sits
behind position sizing.

Responsibilities
- Support portfolio managers with risk measurement and exposure reporting
- Contribute to quantitative research on systematic strategies and signal decay
- Build and maintain tooling in Python; exposure to C++ is a plus
- Communicate analytical results clearly to investment professionals

Qualifications

- Coursework in finance, economics, mathematics, statistics, or computer science
- Demonstrated quantitative ability and comfort working with data
- Strong written and verbal communication
